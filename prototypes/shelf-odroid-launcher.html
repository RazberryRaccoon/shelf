Shelf

A launcher prototype for the Odroid Go, drawn pixel for pixel at the device’s 320×240 resolution.

Controls

←→
Browse cartridges (hold to scroll faster)
↑↓
Switch shelves: NES, Game Boy, Game Boy Color
X
A: open a game, choose
Z
B: back
Shift
Select: favorite
Enter
Start: change sort order
Esc
Menu: settings
V
Volume

Leave it idle for 20 seconds to see attract mode. Sound starts after your first button press. The box art is generated placeholder art; your real covers go on these cartridges.

Play a game, then press B to quit: it saves your spot, and the game’s page offers Resume from then on.
